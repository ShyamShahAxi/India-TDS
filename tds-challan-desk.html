        Other bank debits

        Vendor rules

        Each invoice takes the first rule whose vendor text appears in the vendor name. Rules with description text are checked first, so one vendor can be split (Indiqube rent and services). A lower rate applies a certificate from its valid-from date up to its limit. Changes are saved in this browser.

        Section codes (Income-tax Act, 2025)

        Codes follow the advisor's April–August summaries: 1023 contractors who are individuals or HUFs (1%), 1024 other contractors (2%), 1026 technical fees (2%), 1027 professional fees (10%), 1008 equipment rent, 1009 property rent. SWAI's certificate places professional fees at section 393(1), Table Sl. No. 6(iii).D(b). Confirm before filing.
